What you will learn

From switching it on to sewing your first seam.

  • Identify the essential parts of your sewing machine
  • Wind and insert a bobbin correctly
  • Thread the upper portion of your machine
  • Sew a secure, even basic seam
  • Understand common stitches and settings
  • Choose appropriate needles and thread
  • Clean your machine and solve common problems

Before you begin

Gather a few basics.

You do not need a large collection of tools. Start with what you have and check your machine manual whenever its instructions differ from the course.

Your sewing machine Any modern domestic machine
Your machine manual A digital copy works too
All-purpose thread Use new, good-quality thread
Correct bobbins Made for your machine model
Scrap woven fabric Quilting cotton is ideal
Basic sewing tools Scissors, pins or clips, and a ruler
