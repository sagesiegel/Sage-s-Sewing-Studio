Before you begin

Gather a few basics.

You do not need a large collection of tools. Start with what you have, and check your machine manual whenever its instructions differ from the course.

  • 01 Your sewing machine Any modern domestic machine
  • 02 Your machine manual A digital copy works too
  • 03 All-purpose thread Use new, good-quality thread
  • 04 Correct bobbins Made for your machine model
  • 05 Scrap woven fabric Quilting cotton is ideal
  • 06 Basic sewing tools Scissors, pins or clips, and a ruler
